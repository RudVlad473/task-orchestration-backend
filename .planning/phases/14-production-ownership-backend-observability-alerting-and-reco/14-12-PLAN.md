<data_flow>
Each diagram is a Mermaid source rendered to PNG by a digest-pinned mermaid-cli container, and verify-diagrams.py checks the committed PNGs against their sources and the manifest's scale. </data_flow>

- docs/wiki/ copies are not authoritative; only the originals are edited.
- The measured dead-man time comes from 14-11, which may finish after this plan; until then the caveat text names D-06's 10-15 minute expectation and points to the runbook section 14-11 writes.
</approaches>
