<data_flow>
Prometheus, inside the cluster, calls the app pod's port 9081 at /actuator/prometheus through the PodMonitor `app`; that port is a second Tomcat connector that the Service and the IngressRoutes never reference, so the internet cannot reach it. Non-obvious trade-offs:
- Spring Session JDBC: without SessionCreationPolicy.STATELESS on the management chain, a scrape every 30s per pod could create session rows; the chain is stateless on purpose.
- Every RANDOM_PORT test context now starts a second Tomcat; a small, fixed startup cost per cached context.
- Widening exposure to prometheus is safe only while 9081 stays unrouted; the prohibition in must_haves is the guard, and T3 checks the live Service.
- Probes keep today's aggregate semantics (the db indicator is in /actuator/health), so a database outage still restarts the pod through liveness, exactly as today; changing probe semantics is not part of this phase.
- The public /api/healthz is a db-only group: it answers "can the app reach its database", not the full aggregate.
</approaches>
