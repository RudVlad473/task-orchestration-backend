## Non-obvious trade-offs
- **Performance:** the PNG export of about 20 views runs about three minutes (research measured 10 views in 1 min 23 s); it is run once, in Task 3.
- **State invalidation:** host-level steps (Cloud Firewall, the svclb DNAT) rest on doc claims (research A4); they are labelled authored in the evidence, so a later reader knows they are unverifiable from the repo.
- **Security:** the push-to-image steps run through deploy.yml, which holds secret references and an SSH forward; quotes are chosen from lines without them. Phase 4's leak scan of the built site is the backstop.
</tradeoffs>
