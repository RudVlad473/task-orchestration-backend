== readability judgement for the development view (I opened each PNG with the Read tool)
Limit of this judgement: the Read tool shows the image downscaled (the first one 8950x5272 shown at 2000x1178). I did not view
any of them at natural size, and I had no tool to crop one (no PIL, no ImageMagick on this host). Labels and arrows were judged
from the downscaled image.

The five traces, from generated.c4's edge list (sorted):
1. first edge, activitylog -> constant. From the right side of activitylog a long arc sweeps right and down to the right side of
   constant, arrow at constant. Traceable, but it runs next to two other long arcs into constant (controller -> constant and
   handler -> constant), so the target is identified by following the arc, not by reading its end. PASS with care.
2. last edge, service -> repository. A near-vertical line from the top of service to the bottom of repository, arrow at
   repository. PASS, unambiguous.
3. service -> config. A short diagonal from the upper left of service to the lower right of config, arrow at config. PASS.
4. service -> mapper. A short diagonal from the lower left of service to the top right of mapper, arrow at mapper. PASS.
5. service -> exception. A direct line from the right side of service to the left side of exception, arrow at exception. PASS.
   (service -> entity, -> event, -> dto and -> constant were also followed and are distinguishable.)

Not unambiguous, and not one of the five traces: the three arcs that meet at the left side of entity (base -> entity, dto -> entity
and entity -> base run as near-parallel curves, and only the tiny arrowheads say which way each points), and the eight arrows that
arrive at constant. Edges carry no label by design, so direction rests on the arrowhead alone.

Verdict for the default layout (TopBottom, no autoLayout rule): all 14 package labels are legible in the downscaled image and all
five traces are unambiguous, so it meets the plan's rule for readable. It is readable as a graph of 14 boxes and 38 arrows only in
the sense that single edges can be followed; reading the whole dependency structure from it, for example seeing that eight
packages form one cycle, is not possible from the picture (the cycle line printed by sync.mjs check says it). The 8-package
cycle is visible as arrows pointing both ways between entity, config, dto, base, service and security, not as a grouping.

== layouts tried (the real model keeps the default; the two others were rendered from scratch copies via scratch/layout.fish)
A. default (TopBottom, spacing chosen by LikeC4): 8950 x 5272, natural 4475 x 2636. Five traces as above. KEPT.
B. autoLayout LeftRight 120 110: 7792 x 8938, natural 3896 x 4469. Verdict: worse. Edges run as large arcs around the whole
   graph (handler and controller edges to constant loop across the top), eight arrows converge on constant, and the entity/base
   pair becomes two long parallel arcs along the bottom. Trace 1 (activitylog -> constant) goes round the left and top of the
   graph and is hard to follow; traces 2 to 5 stay short and clear. Rejected.
C. autoLayout TopBottom 200 160: 12500 x 6552, natural 6250 x 3276. Same topology as A (same box order and the same arcs),
   only farther apart, so nothing became clearer and the picture grew 1.4 times wider. Edges drew dotted here (the spec gives
   kind imports `line dotted`); in A they looked solid at the downscale I viewed, and I did not establish why. Rejected.

Not tried: grouping the packages into layers. The standard calls that a possibility, not a result, and the plan says not to invent
a grouping.
